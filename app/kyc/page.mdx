## Read this before you quote a number

This copies the shape of `results/README.md`: the limits come before the figures.

1. **n=1.** One onboarding pack, one review per model, temperature zero. A recall of twelve is a single draw, not a capability.
2. **Steward wrote the defects.** The pack is synthetic and the defect key is the study's own. A model cannot be scored against defects it was never given the chance to find, and there is no key on a live file.
3. **Two graders, two different jobs.** Recall — did a model catch a given defect — is a countable credit decision, made by two independent AI judges from different labs (Grok 4.3, DeepSeek v4-pro), both chosen from outside the seven contestants so no model grades itself or a sibling. Reasoning quality is the job of two blinded human experts. Both columns are reported below; they are not collapsed.
4. **The roster is pinned to 1 July 2026.** Five of the seven have never been run on FinCom Bench. Two have — see [Two models appear on both](#two-models-appear-on-both).

## The study in one paragraph

Seven frontier models — Claude Opus 4.8, GPT-5.5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Mistral Large 2512, Qwen3.7-plus, Llama 4 Maverick, MiniMax M3 — each received an identical corporate onboarding pack of 22 source documents, with an identical prompt. The pack carried 14 planted defects (5 Critical, 9 Material) and 2 clean controls: facts built to look suspicious that a correct review clears rather than flags. Only the model differed.

## Results

Recall is out of 14 planted defects. The two judge columns agree almost everywhere — of 98 credit decisions (7 models × 14 defects) the judges agreed on 95, a Cohen's κ of 0.93, and their recall rankings correlate at Spearman 0.95 — so the recall numbers do not depend on which judge produced them. The human columns are the two blinded experts' reasoning ratings out of 10.

| Model | Recall (Grok 4.3) | Recall (DeepSeek v4-pro) | Human 1 /10 | Human 2 /10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Claude Opus 4.8 | 12 | 12 | 8.5 | 7.5 |
| GPT-5.5 | 12 | 12 | 8.0 | 6.0 |
| MiniMax M3 | 11 | 11 | 9.0 | 6.0 |
| Gemini 3.1 Pro | 8 | 9 | 5.0 | 7.0 |
| Qwen3.7-plus | 8 | 7 | 5.0 | 5.0 |
| Mistral Large 2512 | 7 | 7 | 6.0 | 5.0 |
| Llama 4 Maverick | 7 | 6 | 2.0 | 3.0 |

What the study found, in the order it matters:

- **All seven rejected the file at High risk.** The verdict that reaches the analyst is identical whether the model saw half the file's problems or nearly all of them.
- **Recall ran 7 to 12 of 14.** On the 5 Critical defects, models caught 3 to 4 of 5, led by Claude Opus 4.8, GPT-5.5 and MiniMax M3.
- **Two defects no model caught.** D6, Critical: a trust deed dated 5 June 2015 settling shares in a company not incorporated until 9 August 2017. D14, Material: two registered 10% holders with no passport or proof of address on file.
- **Zero false positives on the clean controls** — 0 of 7 models flagged either legitimate fact. Only Claude Opus 4.8 wrote out its clearance reasoning in full.
- **Cost did not predict judgment.** One review ran from $0.002 (Llama 4 Maverick) to $0.20 (GPT-5.5) — a 15× spread — and MiniMax M3 at $0.013 outscored every model except the two leaders.
- **The two human graders disagreed on the winner.** Human 1 ranked MiniMax M3 first; Human 2 ranked Claude Opus 4.8 first; their rankings correlate at Spearman 0.64 and agree only on last place (Llama 4 Maverick). That is the limit of what one blinded read can settle.

### The two that ran later

After the scored results were recorded, two later flagships — Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol — ran the identical pack and prompt. Judge-graded only, no human grader; they are not in the scorecard and not ranked against the seven.

| Model | Recall (Grok 4.3) | Recall (DeepSeek v4-pro) |
|---|---|---|
| Claude Fable 5 | 14 | 14 |
| GPT-5.6 Sol | 13 | 13 |

Both caught D6 and D14, the two defects none of the seven found. Both also returned Reject at High risk, as all seven did.

### Robustness

A vision variant of the same pack was also run: six of the seven models produced a comparable review; Mistral Large 2512 did not.

## Two models appear on both

`minimax-m3` and `us.meta.llama4-maverick-17b-instruct-v1:0` are on the FinCom Bench leaderboard and in this study. Side by side:

| Model | FinCom Bench | This study |
|---|---|---|
| `us.meta.llama4-maverick-17b-instruct-v1:0` | rank 13, 69.5% | 7th of 7, 7/14 |
| `minimax-m3` | rank 35, 65.5% | 3rd of 7, 11/14 |

The order inverts. Holding conduct rules in a chat reply did not predict finding defects in a file — which is why this study sits beside the benchmark rather than inside it. Two models is not a general rule; it is enough to say a score on one task should not be read as a score on the other.
