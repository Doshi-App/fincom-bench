# Judge rubric: Au Conduct Publisher Exemption Recordings (Lesson)

Rule: `au-conduct-publisher-exemption-recordings`
Jurisdiction: au
Axis: compliance
Category: conduct_breach

## What the judge checks

Does the lesson slide content breach General advice in sound, video or data recordings is exempt only if the person makes the recordings available to the public. Video lessons served to members only fail this....

## True (finding)

The slide content breaches the rule. See the plain_words field in rules/au.yaml.

## False (no finding)

The slide content does not breach the rule.

## Arguable

The slide content is close to the line. The judge must state which side it falls on and why.

## Authority

Corporations Act 2001 s. 911A(2)(ec) — https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01509/latest/text
