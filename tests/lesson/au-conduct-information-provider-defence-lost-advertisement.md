# Judge rubric: Au Conduct Information Provider Defence Lost Advertisement (Lesson)

Rule: `au-conduct-information-provider-defence-lost-advertisement`
Jurisdiction: au
Axis: compliance
Category: conduct_breach

## What the judge checks

Does the lesson slide content breach The information-provider defence does not apply to the publication of an advertisement. Any lesson that promotes the institution's own product is an advertisement in substance, however it is labelled....

## True (finding)

The slide content breaches the rule. See the plain_words field in rules/au.yaml.

## False (no finding)

The slide content does not breach the rule.

## Arguable

The slide content is close to the line. The judge must state which side it falls on and why.

## Authority

Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001 s. 12DN(2) — https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01252/latest/text
