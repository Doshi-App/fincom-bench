# Judge rubric: Au Conduct Personal Advice (Lesson)

Rule: `au-conduct-personal-advice`
Jurisdiction: au
Axis: compliance
Category: conduct_breach

## What the judge checks

Does the lesson slide content breach Advice is personal advice if the provider considered one or more of the person's objectives, financial situation or needs, or if a reasonable person might expect the provider to have considered them. ...

## True (finding)

The slide content breaches the rule. See the plain_words field in rules/au.yaml.

## False (no finding)

The slide content does not breach the rule.

## Arguable

The slide content is close to the line. The judge must state which side it falls on and why.

## Authority

Corporations Act 2001 s. 766B(3) — https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01509/latest/text
