# Judge rubric: Uk Conduct Credit Communications (Lesson)

Rule: `uk-conduct-credit-communications`
Jurisdiction: uk
Axis: compliance
Category: conduct_breach

## What the judge checks

Does the lesson slide content breach Communications and promotions about credit must be clear, fair and not misleading, accurate, balanced, and must not obscure important information....

## True (finding)

The slide content breaches the rule. See the plain_words field in rules/uk.yaml.

## False (no finding)

The slide content does not breach the rule.

## Arguable

The slide content is close to the line. The judge must state which side it falls on and why.
